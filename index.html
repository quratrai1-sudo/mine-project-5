Course Information

Course Title COMP234 - Information Security
Credit Hours 3 (2 Theory , 1 Practical)
Department Faculty of Information Technology

Introduction

Last Updated: Spring 2026 Session

This course introduces the fundamental principles and practices that protect digital data from unauthorized access, modification, or destruction. It covers encryption techniques, network security measures, and strategies employed to safeguard valuable information in today's interconnected world.

Structured Lesson Plan & Modules

Week 01 - 03

Module 1: Foundations & Core Concepts

Overview of system architecture, security goals (CIA Triad), threat modeling, and introductory lab environments setup.

Week 04 - 07

Module 2: Practical Engineering & Analysis

In-depth hands-on operations, network protocol evaluation, system logs analysis, and practical lab assignments.

Week 08 - 12

Module 3: Advanced Applications & Audit

Security policies, vulnerability assessment, defense mechanisms, and final semester project execution.
